About VISTA
Smarter Surveillance for Safer Public Spaces
VISTA is an AI-based smart surveillance prototype integrating multi-object tracking and person re-identification, developed at Sri Sai Ram Institute of Technology, Chennai. It uses computer vision and deep learning to detect, track, and re-identify individuals in real-time across multiple CCTV streams — even under occlusions or appearance changes.
PythonOpenCVYOLOv8 DeepSORTDeep LearningTensorFlowNumPy
🎯
Real-time Multi-Object Tracking
YOLOv8-based detection combined with DeepSORT assigns persistent unique IDs to every individual across all video frames continuously.
🔄
Cross-Camera Re-Identification
Maintains identity continuity when individuals move between different camera fields of view — even under occlusions or clothing changes.
🚨
Suspicious Behavior Detection
Analyzes anonymous movement patterns and automatically generates real-time alerts for security personnel when anomalies are detected.
📉
Reduced Manual Monitoring
Automates the surveillance workflow — reducing operator workload significantly and enabling faster response to potential threats.
🔒
Privacy-First Architecture
All tracking uses anonymous IDs only. No biometric data is stored or processed. Designed to comply with data protection principles.
⚡
Software-Only Solution
Runs entirely on existing CCTV infrastructure — no hardware changes required. Deployable on standard servers with GPU acceleration.
